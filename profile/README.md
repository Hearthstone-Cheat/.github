### Are Hearthstone cheats allowed?

Blizzard's End User License Agreement prohibits unauthorized cheats, bots, and hacks that modify or facilitate gameplay or provide an unfair advantage.

Yes. Blizzard has publicly confirmed permanent account closures for cheating and unauthorized gameplay automation.

Not automatically. Some tracking applications are permitted in certain contexts, but tournament rules impose specific restrictions. Always check the rules applicable to your game mode or event.
